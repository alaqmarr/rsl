## Choose Where Your Business Files Live

RSL Marketing never locks your business files into a single provider. Open **Backups & Storage** in your sidebar to choose:

- **Bring Your Own Cloudflare R2 or S3 Bucket**: Connect your personal cloud bucket using your endpoint URL, bucket name, and access keys at no extra RSL storage fee.
- **RSL Managed Cloud Storage (Paid Add-On)**: Zero setup required. Request activation in **Backups & Storage** or **Plans & Wallet** to store Tally bill PDFs, media attachments, and backups automatically.

## Switching Storage Providers Without Losing Files

Moving between Managed Cloud Storage and your own Cloudflare R2 / S3 bucket takes one step:

1. Enter your destination bucket details in **Backups & Storage**.
2. Keep **Copy and verify all existing stored files** checked and click **Save Storage Settings**.
3. Every stored file is copied across and verified automatically before switching your active storage destination.

## Full Workspace Backups & Safe Restore

> **Safety Guarantee:** Restoring a backup never erases your **Prepaid Wallet passbook**, **paid GST tax invoices**, or **unsubscribed WhatsApp phone numbers**.

- **One-Click Exports (Always Available)**: Download **Contacts CSV**, **Tally Prime XML**, or **Portable JSON** anytime from the top-right bar of **Backups & Storage**.
- **Password-Protected Backups (`.rslbackup`)**: With the **Encrypted Customer Backups & Export** plan unlocked, create and download full workspace snapshots on demand. Before restoring any archive, RSL Marketing automatically saves a safety snapshot of your current workspace.
