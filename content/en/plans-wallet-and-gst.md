## How Modular Plans vs. Your Prepaid Wallet Work Together

RapidShift Labs separates **predictable software access** from **pay-as-you-go per-message usage** so you never overpay:

| Component | What It Pays For | How It Works |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Feature Plans & Value Bundles** | Unlocks software modules (Tally Sync, WhatsApp Studio, Shared Inbox, Meta Ads, Google Reviews, Storage, Backups, REST API) | Choose **Monthly**, **Quarterly**, or **Yearly** cycle; verified payment unlocks the module and issues an 18% GST Tax Invoice |

## Step 1 — Requesting a Plan or Value Bundle Activation (Minute-by-Minute)

1. Click **Plans & Wallet** (`/billing`) in the left sidebar *(or click **Activate Plan** on any locked feature screen)*.
2. **Check Value Bundles First**: If you need both Tally Prime + WhatsApp + Shared Inbox (or the full Growth Suite), compare the **Value Bundles** row at the top — unlocking a bundle activates all included tools together at a lower combined rate!
3. Click **Activate Plan** (or **Unlock Bundle**) to open the **Payment & Activation Modal**:
   - **Select Billing Cycle**: Click `Monthly`, `Quarterly`, or `Yearly` and review the live **Subtotal**, **18% GST**, and **Grand Total Payable (₹)**.
   - **Pay the Grand Total**:
     - **Via UPI App**: Open Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM, or Corporate UPI on your phone and scan the **Dynamic UPI QR Code** shown inside the modal (the exact rupee amount and payee details are pre-filled automatically!).
     - **Via Bank NEFT / IMPS / RTGS**: Copy the **Beneficiary Name**, **Bank Account Number**, and **IFSC Code** displayed beside the QR code using the **Copy** buttons and transfer the exact Grand Total.
4. **Copy & Paste Your 12-Digit UTR / UPI Reference Number** (see exact app-by-app instructions below!).
5. Paste the UTR into **UTR / Payment Reference Number**, add an optional note (e.g. `Paid from ICICI Current A/c`), and click **Submit Activation Request**.
   - While awaiting finance verification, the button shows **Payment Verification Pending** (preventing accidental duplicate submissions). The moment verification completes, your tools unlock automatically!

## Step 2 — Exact Steps to Find Your 12-Digit UTR Number in Every Payment App

Never paste your phone number or UPI ID (`name@okicici`) as the payment reference — finance verification checks the **12-digit bank UTR / RRN**:

- **Google Pay (GPay)**: Open **Google Pay -> See transaction history** -> tap the payment to RapidShift Labs -> copy the **12-digit `UPI transaction ID`** (for example `430718294012`).
- **PhonePe**: Open **PhonePe -> History** (bottom right) -> tap the transaction -> under **Transfer Details**, tap the copy icon next to the **12-digit `UTR`** number.
- **Paytm**: Open **Paytm -> Balance & History** -> tap the payment -> tap **View Details** -> copy the **12-digit `UPI Ref No`**.
- **IMPS / NEFT / Corporate NetBanking**: Open your payment receipt or SMS alert and copy the **`IMPS Ref No.` (12 digits)** or **`NEFT UTR Number`** (e.g. `HDFCN520261103...`).

## Step 3 — Recharging Your Prepaid Wallet & How `-₹200.00` Overdraft Works

1. Open **Plans & Wallet** (`/billing?tab=wallet`) and scroll to **Recharge Prepaid Wallet**.
2. Enter your desired **Top-Up Amount (₹)** (for example `1000`, `2500`, or `5000`).
3. Scan the UPI QR code or remit via IMPS/NEFT, paste your **12-digit UTR**, choose payment method (`UPI` or `Bank Transfer`), and click **Submit Wallet Recharge**.

### Understanding Your `-₹200.00` Overdraft Credit Buffer

- Suppose your wallet has **`₹12.00`** left at 7:30 PM while your accountant saves 40 Tally GST sales invoices (`₹0.90` each = `₹36.00`).
- **Without overdraft**, bills #14–#40 would pause. **With RapidShift's `-₹200.00` buffer**, all 40 customer GST invoices go out immediately and your wallet moves smoothly to **`-₹24.00`** (amber **Overdraft** pill in your top bar, with `₹176.00` remaining buffer down to `-₹200.00`).
- Metered per-message sends pause **only** if the balance reaches **`-₹200.00` (`Limit Reached`)**.
- On your next verified `₹1,000.00` recharge, `-₹24.00 + ₹1,000.00` automatically settles to **`+₹976.00`**, with every single credit and debit recorded permanently in your **Wallet Passbook** (downloadable anytime via **Download Passbook Statement PDF**).

## Step 4 — Downloading & Printing Rule 46 GST Tax Invoices (`SAC 998314`)

1. Open **Plans & Wallet** (`/billing`) and scroll to **Official GST Tax Invoices**.
2. Make sure your **15-character GSTIN** and **State Code** were saved in **Business Settings** (`/settings/business`):
   - **Maharashtra (`27`)**: Shows **9% CGST + 9% SGST**.
   - **All Other States**: Shows **18% IGST**.
3. Click **View / Print Tax Invoice** on any row to open `/billing/invoices/<id>`, then click **Print / Save PDF** (or **Download Official PDF**) for your GST GSTR-2B / accounting records.
