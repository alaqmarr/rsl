## How Feature Plans & Activation Work

Instead of forcing every business onto an expensive all-in-one bundle, RapidShift Labs (`rapidshiftlabs.in`) offers modular monthly capabilities across **Customer Messages**, **Tally Prime**, **WhatsApp**, **Social & Ads**, **Google Reviews**, and **Developer API**.

1. **Inspect Full Plan Details**: Open any feature page in your sidebar or visit **Plans & Wallet** to see every included capability, monthly rate, per-unit usage rate, and 18% GST breakdown.
2. **Submit a Feature Activation Request**: Enter your optional UPI / Bank UTR payment reference or note and click **Request Feature Activation**.
3. **Payment Verification & Instant Unlock**: Once our billing team verifies and records your payment, your feature page unlocks immediately and a paid GST tax invoice appears in **Plans & Wallet**.

> **Important:** Feature pages stay locked until payment is verified and recorded on your account.

## Your Prepaid Message Wallet & -₹200.00 Overdraft Buffer

Your **Prepaid Wallet** funds per-message and per-request usage (such as WhatsApp Business utility/marketing templates, automatic Tally PDF invoice sends, broadcast campaigns, and smart review reply drafts) and works like an append-only passbook:

- **Uninterrupted `-₹200.00` Overdraft Credit**: If your wallet runs low during busy billing hours, WhatsApp messages and metered per-request actions continue working automatically down to **`-₹200.00`** (`-20,000` paise). Your negative balance (for example `-₹85.50`) and remaining overdraft credit are shown clearly in your top bar, sidebar, and **Plans & Wallet**.
- **Automatic Recovery on Next Recharge**: Submit a **UPI / Bank Transfer UTR** in **Plans & Wallet** anytime — once verified, the recharge clears your negative balance first and credits the remainder immediately. Past passbook entries are permanent and can never be edited or deleted.

## Official 18% GST Tax Invoices

Every verified plan payment and wallet recharge generates a sequential GST tax invoice (for example `RSL/26-27/0001`) under **SAC `998314`**:
