## How WhatsApp's 24-Hour Reply Window Works

When a customer sends your business a message on WhatsApp, you have **24 hours** to reply freely with regular text or attachments inside **Customer Messages**. Once 24 hours pass since their last reply, WhatsApp requires an **approved template message** (such as a Tally bill update, payment receipt, or festival offer).

> **Good to Know:** Our shared inbox automatically tracks the 24-hour customer service window for every conversation and prompts you to pick an approved template whenever needed.

## Building Official WhatsApp Business Templates

1. Open **WhatsApp & Templates** in your sidebar (once **WhatsApp Business Messaging** is active on your account).
2. Click **Load Ready-Made Tally Templates** for instant Sales Bill, Payment Receipt, and Overdue Reminder templates — or design a custom template using the **Visual Template Builder**.
3. Map variables like `{{1}}` Customer Name, `{{2}}` Bill Number, `{{3}}` Amount in ₹, and `{{4}}` Due Date with a live mobile phone preview on the right.
4. Click **Sync Templates with WhatsApp** to submit or refresh approval status directly from your connected WhatsApp Business Account.

## Sending Bulk Broadcasts Safely

1. Record customer permission in **WhatsApp Opt-In Directory** so you only message customers who agreed to hear from your business.
2. Select an approved template and review the audience count and per-message wallet rate before launching.
3. Unsubscribed phone numbers are skipped automatically before a single rupee is spent from your Prepaid Wallet.

## Permanent Unsubscribe Protection

Whenever a customer replies `STOP` or `UNSUBSCRIBE`, their phone number is immediately added to your permanent **Unsubscribed List**. Even if you restore an older workspace backup later, your unsubscribed list stays intact so opted-out customers are never messaged accidentally.
