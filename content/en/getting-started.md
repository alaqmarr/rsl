## Welcome to RSL Marketing

RSL Marketing brings your **customer messages**, **Tally Prime sales bills**, **WhatsApp Business templates**, **social ad campaigns**, and **Google Business reviews** together into one calm, reliable workspace.

> **Dedicated Business Workspace:** Every figure on your dashboard comes directly from your actual bills, payment receipts, and customer conversations — never estimated or sample numbers.

## Five Steps to Launch Your Workspace

1. **Confirm Your Business Profile & GSTIN**: Open **Team & Business** to enter your GSTIN and billing state so all official tax invoices calculate **9% CGST + 9% SGST** (same state) or **18% IGST** (inter-state) accurately.
2. **Review Plans & Request Feature Activation**: Open **Plans & Wallet** (or any feature tab) to review everything included in each plan and submit a **Feature Activation Request**. Once payment is verified and recorded, your feature unlocks automatically along with a GST tax invoice.
3. **Top Up Your Prepaid Message Wallet**: Submit a UPI / Bank UTR transfer or online payment in **Plans & Wallet** to fund per-message WhatsApp template and broadcast usage.
4. **Connect Your Channels**: Open **Connected Accounts** to link your official WhatsApp Business number, Facebook & Instagram Pages, or Google Business Profile.
5. **Connect Tally Prime & Automate Bills**: Load the 1-click Tally Prime `.tdl` add-on, use your personal Tally Webhook URL, or upload Tally XML / CSV exports in [Tally Prime Sync & Payment Reminders](/guides/tally-sync-and-import).
